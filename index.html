Quick & Delicious Recipes

My favorite recipes that you can prepare quickly with few ingredients

Garlic Herb Chicken Breast

⏱️ 25 Minutes

Ingredients:

  • 250g chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Thyme, paprika, black pepper, salt

Instructions:

Slice the chicken thinly and toss with olive oil and spices. Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for about 20 minutes.

Red Lentil Soup

⏱️ 30 Minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1 medium onion
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste and butter
  • 5 cups hot water

Instructions:

Sauté the chopped onion in butter and stir in the tomato paste. Add the washed lentils and hot water, simmer until tender, then blend until smooth.
